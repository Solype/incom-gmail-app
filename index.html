INCOM Gmail App

INCOM Gmail App is an internal tool operated by INCOM Services, a fleet management company for VTC (ride-hailing) drivers in France.

Twice a day, it reads the unread emails of the INCOM operations mailbox (read-only access) to detect notifications from ride-hailing and rental platforms (Bolt, Uber, Sixt, LeCab) about driver or vehicle documents that are expiring, expired or rejected. For each such notification, it posts an alert to INCOM's internal Slack workspace so the team can contact the driver in time.

The app never sends, modifies, deletes or marks emails as read. It is used only by INCOM Services staff for INCOM's own mailbox.
