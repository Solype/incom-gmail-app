Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Data accessed

The App uses the gmail.readonly scope on a single mailbox owned by INCOM Services. It reads unread emails to find document notifications. No other Google data is accessed.

Use, storage and sharing

Email content is processed only to send internal alerts to INCOM staff. It is not stored, sold or shared with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

INCOM Gmail App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
