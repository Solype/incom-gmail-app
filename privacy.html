Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy describes how INCOM Gmail App (“the App”), operated by INCOM Services (“INCOM”, “we”), accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

1. Data we access

The App requests a single Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. It is authorized only on INCOM's own operations mailbox. With this scope, the App reads:

  • for each unread email received since the previous run: sender, subject, date received and the short preview (snippet) provided by Gmail;
  • the body of emails sent by Bolt, Uber, Sixt and LeCab, to extract the vehicle licence plate, driver name and document type they mention.

The App does not access contacts, attachments, calendar, Drive or any other Google service.

2. How we use the data

The data is used only to detect platform notifications about driver or vehicle documents that are expiring, expired or rejected, to match them with the driver or vehicle in INCOM's own management system, and to post an alert to INCOM's internal Slack workspace. Emails that are not such notifications are ignored.

3. Storage and retention

  • Email content is processed in memory and is not stored in a database.
  • The App stores the time of its last successful run, so the next run only reads newer emails.
  • Technical logs (sender, subject and snippet of the platform notifications that triggered an alert) are kept in INCOM's private Amazon Web Services account for troubleshooting, with access limited to INCOM technical staff.
  • The OAuth credentials are stored encrypted in AWS Secrets Manager.

4. Sharing

Alerts derived from the emails (platform, document type, deadline, licence plate, driver name and status) are posted to a private channel of INCOM's Slack workspace, visible only to INCOM staff. We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with any other third party. Our service providers (Amazon Web Services for hosting, Slack for internal messaging) process the data only on our behalf.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

INCOM Gmail App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is not used for advertising, is not transferred to data brokers, is not read by humans except for security, troubleshooting or legal compliance, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS only. Credentials are encrypted at rest. Access to the infrastructure is restricted to authorized INCOM staff.

7. Your rights and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal data concerning you by writing to the contact address below.
